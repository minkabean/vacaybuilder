Why Martha's Vineyard?

For generations, Martha's Vineyard—and especially Oak Bluffs—has been a meaningful summer destination for Black families, artists, leaders, and communities. Learn about the island, its history, Inkwell Beach, the African American Heritage Trail, and why this celebration belongs here.
