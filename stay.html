Lodging

Where to stay

Guests will choose and book their own accommodations.

Options from Trina's notes

  • Narragansett — 1 bedroom
  • Pequot — 3 bedroom
  • The Oak Bluffs Inn — 2 bedroom
  • Summer Camp — another popular Oak Bluffs option mentioned in her notes

Rates can change, so this first version intentionally does not present the 2029 estimates as live pricing.

Oak Bluffs is the easiest home base

Trina notes that there are many places to stay across the island, but getting to Oak Bluffs from other towns can be more difficult in August.
