Explore Martha's Vineyard

Special events, coffee, beaches, shopping, culture, and other ideas from Trina's planning document.

Special events and happenings

  • Legacy Week — worship service; Rich Aunties & Rose Brunch.
  • African American Film Festival — screenings, panels, and celebrity sightings.
  • Comedy Fest.
  • Grand Illumination Night — Gingerbread Cottages lit up.
  • Black Fashion Weekend.
  • Vibe Fest.
  • Black Book Fest.
  • Fairs, concerts, and road races.

Coffee shops

  • Mocha Mott's — Circuit Ave.
  • Aalia's — Kennebec Ave.
  • Tocco Puro Coffee — Circuit Ave.
  • Highlands General.

Island ideas at a glance

  • Inkwell Beach, South Beach, and State Beach.
  • Oak Bluffs Gingerbread Cottages.
  • African American Heritage Trail.
  • Aquinnah Cliffs and Gay Head Lighthouse.
  • Vineyard Haven shopping.
  • Edgartown and Vineyard Haven galleries.
  • Bike rides, beach yoga, the Polar Bears, spa time, and sunset sailing.
