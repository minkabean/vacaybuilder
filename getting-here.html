Travel

Getting here

Important Travel Note

Martha's Vineyard is an island.

Your trip has two parts:

1Travel to Massachusetts
2Take the ferry to Oak Bluffs

Arrival · August 5, 2029

  • Fly into Boston, Massachusetts.
  • Use Uber from the airport to the ferry.
  • Take the ferry from Hyannis, Massachusetts to Oak Bluffs.
  • Check in to lodging on August 5.

Return · August 18, 2029

  • Check out of lodging.
  • Take the ferry from Oak Bluffs to Hyannis.
  • Fly home to O'Hare.

Getting around the island

  • Some guests may rent bikes.
  • The bus is described in Trina's notes as dependable.
  • Ubers may be less reliable, especially in August.
  • Transportation back to Oak Bluffs from other island towns can be harder in August.

Luggage storage

If you arrive before hotel check-in or want to stay on the island after checkout, Trina's notes list Dockside Inn for luggage storage at $10 per bag for the day, across from Sandbar.
