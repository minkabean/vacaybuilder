Travel

Getting here

Martha's Vineyard is an island, so your trip includes both mainland travel and a ferry crossing.

Arrival plan

  • Fly into Boston, Massachusetts — August 5, 2029
  • Travel from the airport to the ferry
  • Take the ferry from Hyannis to Oak Bluffs
  • Check in to your hotel or other lodging

Return plan

  • Oak Bluffs to Hyannis by ferry — August 18, 2029
  • Return flight on August 18, 2029

Reserve early

Trina's planning notes emphasize booking hotels, flights, festival passes, ferries, and some restaurants well in advance.
