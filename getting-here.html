Travel

Getting here

Martha's Vineyard is an island, so your trip includes a ferry crossing.

Arriving

  • Fly into Boston, Massachusetts — August 5, 2029
  • Travel from the airport to the ferry
  • Take the ferry from Hyannis to Oak Bluffs
  • Check in to your lodging

Returning home

  • Oak Bluffs to Hyannis by ferry — August 18, 2029
  • Return flight — August 18, 2029

Plan ahead

Flights and ferry reservations should be made well before the trip.
