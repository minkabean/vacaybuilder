Guest ideas

Ideas for your days on the island

These are suggestions, not a fixed schedule. Guests will be able to add ideas, see who is interested, and make plans together.

Day 1 · Arrive & get acquainted

  • Arrive in Oak Bluffs and check into lodging.
  • Tony's Market — all-day breakfast and sandwiches.
  • Walk through the Oak Bluffs Gingerbread Cottages and end at the harbor for sunset.
  • Sweet Life Cafe — make reservations.
  • Back Door Donuts — late-night fresh warm donuts; expect a line.
  • The Ritz Cafe or The Loft — live music and chill crowds.

Day 2 · Black-owned businesses & beach day

  • Biscuits — chicken & waffles or French toast.
  • African American Heritage Trail — a book tour.
  • Menemsha Hills — hiking idea.
  • Cousin Rose Gallery.
  • The Mariposa Museum and World Culture Center.
  • Vineyard Caribbean Cuisine — Jamaican to-go spot.
  • State Beach / JAWS bridge.
  • Donavan's Reef Beach Bar.
  • Nancy's or Lookout Tavern for sunset and dinner.
  • Circuit Ave.; Eleven has themed nights.
  • Midnight Taco — calmer than Circuit Ave.

Day 3 · Adventure & explore the island

  • Tocco Puro — coffee or matcha.
  • Aquinnah Cliffs and Gay Head Lighthouse.
  • Farm Neck Club — golf and food.
  • Honorary Tavern — Jamaican cuisine.
  • Vineyard Haven shops, including Lennox & Harvey.
  • State Road or L'étoile for dinner.
  • Martha's Vineyard Film Center — check screening times ahead.
  • The Strand Theatre — check for comedy events.
  • Sunset sail with MV Charter Co.

Day 4 · Black Joy Day

  • Behind the Bookstore in Vineyard Haven.
  • C'est LaVie for HBCU MV apparel.
  • Island Life Studio for Deb Santos custom designs.
  • Black Dog for classic Martha's Vineyard souvenirs.
  • Giordano's for pizza/snacks.
  • Fat Ronnies for burgers/sandwiches.
  • South Beach.
  • Winston's Kitchen, Atria, or Alchemy for dinner.
  • Noman's Friday–Sunday, 6–9 p.m. — DJ and dancing under the stars.
  • Circuit Ave.

Day 5 · Slow down & relax

  • Vineyard Haven shops.
  • Bike from Oak Bluffs to Edgartown or Vineyard Haven using the paths.
  • Caleen's Day Spa in Vineyard Haven.
  • Shopping and galleries in Edgartown or Vineyard Haven.
  • Rhone Botanics & Skincare.
  • Soul Gems by Maria.
  • Black Joy Kitchen brunch — Sundays, 10 a.m.–4 p.m.
  • Quick dip at Inkwell Beach.
