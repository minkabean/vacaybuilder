Planning

Before you go

The practical details Trina wants everyone to know before arriving.

Book early

  • Hotel — Trina recommends planning about 11 months ahead
  • Flights — months ahead
  • African American Film Festival — about 6 months ahead
  • Ferries — months ahead
  • Some restaurants — months ahead

Pack

  • Sunscreen and bug spray
  • Jacket
  • Beach bag
  • Swimsuit and swim shoes
  • Walking shoes
  • Yoga mat
  • Walkie-talkies
  • A nice all-white outfit, just in case

Island tips

  • Get groceries when you arrive if that works for your schedule
  • Some food spots may be cash only
  • The bus is dependable; Trina's notes say Ubers can be less reliable
  • Internet service can be unreliable in some areas
  • Make dining and activity reservations ahead of time
