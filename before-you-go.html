Planning

Before you go

Booking timing, packing, groceries, internet, transportation, and other practical notes from Trina's document.

Reserve in advance

  • Hotel — about 11 months in advance.
  • Flights — months in advance.
  • African American Film Festival — about 6 months in advance.
  • Ferries — months in advance.
  • Some restaurants — months in advance.

Trina's note is emphatic: decide how many days you want to stay and start saving early.

Groceries and cash

  • Consider getting groceries after getting off the ferry on the way to the hotel.
  • Some food spots are cash only.
  • Trina's notes suggest grocery prices may be better in Edgartown or Vineyard Haven than on Circuit Ave. in Oak Bluffs.

What to pack

  • Sunscreen.
  • Bug spray — Trina specifically noted Avon Skin So Soft.
  • Jackets.
  • Beach bag.
  • Swimsuit and swim shoes.
  • Walking shoes.
  • Yoga mat.
  • Walkie-talkies.
  • A nice all-white outfit, just in case you decide to attend an all-white party.

Internet and meeting up

  • Internet service can be unreliable and varies by location.
  • Tell people where you are going ahead of time so you can still meet up if signals fail.

Dining and activities

Make dining and activity reservations ahead of time whenever possible.
